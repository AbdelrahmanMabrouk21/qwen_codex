The Essence of Sauvage

"Sauvage is a composition with powerful freshness, directed by François Demachy."

Noble raw materials are born of a generous nature. A powerful freshness, directed by François Demachy. An ambergris note, woody, precious, warm, sensual and deep.

The name Sauvage evokes wide-open spaces, a blue sky cloak over a landscape composed of red rocks, scorching in the sunlight.

Top Notes

Calabrian Bergamot, Pepper

Heart Notes

Lavender, Sichuan Pepper, Vetiver

Base Notes

Ambergris, Cedar, Labdanum
